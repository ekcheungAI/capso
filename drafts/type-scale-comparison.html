The left panel has no entry point. Because the page title, the section heading, the card titles and the metadata are all within 3px of each other, the eye lands nowhere and scans everything at equal weight. The report calls Minimalism 2.0 explicitly typography-led — "typography and spacing doing most of the visual work." Capso currently asks colour and spacing to do all of it alone.
