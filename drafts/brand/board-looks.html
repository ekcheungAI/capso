Same product, same content, four colour worlds. No green on cream — you were right that it was reading institutional rather than calm.

First, a correction. My initial five came back as invented coffee brands rather than Capso — one was a Nespresso-compatible capsule shop, another a coffee subscription. Two also rendered real third-party logos: Vercel, Loom, Retool, Ramp, Discord in one; Kinfolk, Monocle and Wallpaper* in another.

Those are unusable at any fidelity and I have not included them. The four below are re-run with the brand name pinned and third-party marks explicitly excluded.

Where I land

Bone & ink for the product. Warm clay for everything you photograph. They are the same family — one is simply lit differently — so a landing page can open in clay and hand off to bone as you scroll into the app.

The through-line across all four worth noticing: every one of them independently used a small coloured dot per capture. That is the intent system already shipping in INTENT_COLOR, and it survives every colour world here. It is the part of this that is genuinely settled.

These are generated compositions, not final designs — the type is placeholder and the interface is indicative. What is real is the colour, the register and the layout logic.
