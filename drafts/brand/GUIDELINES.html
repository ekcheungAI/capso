Brand guidelines

Everything decided, in one place. Version 1 — the working system, not a finished manual.

The feeling

You're not organised. Capso is. The brand has to hold two things at once: competent enough that you hand over your filing without worrying, warm enough that it never feels like it is scolding you for being messy.

Everything below follows from that. Cold technical design fails the second half. Cute design fails the first. The resolution is quiet order — restraint that reads as care rather than austerity.

Product. Bone ground, ink type, no accent colour. The only hue on screen belongs to your captures.

Marketing. Same family, warmer light. Real material, deeper ground, the register everything photographed lives in.

Logo

A capsule lid, seen from above

Three concentric parts, exactly what you see looking down into a rack: the flange rim, the crimp ring, and the sealed foil face. The notch through the right is the finger relief on a real holder — and it opens the ring into a C.

Drawn on a 24-unit grid. Rim r10.4, crimp r8.2, face r6.0, notch y10.4–13.6. One file, currentColor, transparent ground.

Why top-down matters

A medicine capsule is a symmetric oblong seen from the side. A coffee capsule is a truncated cone with a flanged rim — widest at the lid, tapering to the base. The taper is the entire difference. Use the lid as the mark; the side profile exists for the rack, columns and motion.

Retrieval

The rack is only half the promise. Everything above is about order — filing, shelves, things landing where they belong. But the brief was "even though you are not organised, AI will help you organise and make sure you never lose a single train of thought." Order is the first clause. The second is that the right thing comes back.

The rack holds everything. Capso knows which one to pull.

You saved three pricing pages with annual toggles. Two put the discount on the toggle itself; the one you called out as clearest put it on the plan card.

Agentic search over memory already ships (8055391). The register is the rest of the product: same 14px system sans, no chat bubble, no typing animation, no gradient border. The mark is the only thing that says this was written by Capso.

  • Screenshots are the citation. Every claim carries its source capture as a thumbnail you can click. Nothing is asserted without one.
  • Thinking is the crimp-ring pulse — --dur-panel, the one looping motion we allow. Not a spinner, not a shimmer, not three dots.
  • Nothing found says nothing found, in one sentence, and does not guess.

Rules

Do

  • Let captures carry the colour — the interface stays bone and ink
  • Use the italic, not bold or colour, to carry emphasis
  • Keep the mark's states tied to real product states
  • Show empty slots — an unfilled ring is information
  • Photograph objects in order, warm light, real material
  • Set marketing in Fraunces, the app in system sans
  • Mark what Capso decided, and only what Capso decided
  • Give every resurfaced capture a stated reason

Don't

  • No mascot, no face, no eyes. Ever
  • No accent colour on buttons — buttons are ink
  • Never use intent colours as text, only as dots
  • No gradients, no glow, no sparkles for AI — the mark is the signal
  • Never put Fraunces inside the product UI
  • No side-profile capsule as the mark — that reads as medicine
  • Never use the mark as a watermark or empty-state decoration

One source of truth

Everything above is only a brand if it cannot drift. The values live in packages/shared/src/tokens.json and nothing else in the repo may declare a colour. pnpm brand:tokens compiles that file into every surface; pnpm brand:check fails the build if any output is stale or if a source file declares a colour of its own, and it runs as part of pnpm lint.

SurfaceConsumes
apps/webapp/tokens.generated.css, imported by globals.css
apps/extensiontokens.generated.css, linked from popup + options
apps/macsrc/tokens.generated.css, imported by App.css
icons & OG cardtokens.generated.json, read by the Python builders

This exists because the alternative was already failing. The palette had been hand-copied into six places and three had drifted: the extension options page still carried a retired accent pair, the extension popup carried the accent before that plus the OS system colours, and the Mac popover declared no brand values at all — so the surface a user touches most often was the least branded one.

Nobody made a mistake. There was no mechanism, so drift was the default state rather than an error. A brand that depends on everyone remembering is not a system.

One escape hatch — brand-allow: <reason>, with a mandatory written reason — because a blanket ban would have forced code to lie. The sample-capture canvas in capture.tsx depicts somebody else's product, and a fake screenshot that looks like Capso is worse than no sample at all.

The useful side effect: changing the ground is now four values in one file. App, extension, Mac popover, favicon, .icns, tray template and share card all regenerate, and the check proves nothing was missed.

--danger came out of branding the extension, which needed an error colour. No single red clears 4.5:1 on both grounds — the same trap the terracotta accent fell into — so it is a light/dark pair. It is not the ux_bug intent dot: that one is tuned to 3:1 because it is a dot, and this is text.

The mode-dependent accent is gone: ink inverts with the theme, so dark mode is now the plain inverse instead of a hand-tuned second pair.

One consequence worth recording. Roughly seventy call sites used text-accent. Most were fills, rings and borders and simply became ink — but eleven used the hue as the only at-rest signal on a link or control. With the accent equal to the foreground those would have rendered as plain body text.

Those eleven moved to underline underline-offset-2, or to hover:underline where the hover was the affordance. That is the correct answer in an ink-only system, and it is the rule going forward: links are underlined, not coloured. Sites already sitting on text-muted were left alone — muted rising to ink on hover is still a real change.

Shipped

The mark is installed everywhere it belongs, all of it generated from the one SVG: favicon.ico (with the hinted 16px master embedded verbatim), the PWA and Apple touch icons, the four extension sizes, the Tauri .icns/.ico set and the macOS menu-bar template. Plate is bone, mark is ink — an ink plate sits at almost exactly the luminance of Chrome's dark tab strip and disappears into it.

Fraunces is SIL OFL 1.1 — commercial use, embedding and redistribution all permitted. It is a Reserved Font Name, so a modified cut may not be called Fraunces; we do not modify it. That question is closed.
