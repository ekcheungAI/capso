Capso — mascot concepts, round 2

Round one was safe and forgettable. This is a wider concept space, generated expressively rather than reduced to death, with one direction that actually says what the product does.

1 Aug 2026Recraft V4.1 · standard + vector 6 mechanisms · 20 rendersNo files under apps/ touched

Why round one failed

The mark was a rounded blob with two dots — the single most crowded shape in software branding. It had no distinctive element: no silhouette idea, no negative-space device, no unexpected colour relationship.

The blunt test: could that identity be renamed for another brand without meaningful changes? Yes. It could be Headspace, Calm, or any wellness app. That is a fail.

The cause was my prompting, not the model. Every round-one prompt said minimal, extremely simple, must read at 16px, locked to three colours in model_type: vector. I optimised for reduction and got something reduced to nothing. Concept first, reduce afterwards.

Central mechanism

The capsule is transparent, and you can see everything you captured inside it.

One idea that generates the mark, the mascot, the colour system, and the motion — rather than four decisions that merely coexist. The shell stays terracotta and constant; the contents carry all the variety. That is the product: one container, many captured things.

The system

Same object at four scales.

Mark

Three cards, hard stop. The cream window is the distinctive element — no other capsule mark has visible cargo.

Character

Packed dense with hundreds of cards. Same object, more detail — used large, never shrunk to icon size.

Project system

One capsule per thread — different hue, different contents. The identity scales with the product instead of repeating.

Motion — capture and retrieval

Capsule → dissolve → reassemble. Note the third frame: the pixels reform into a grid, which is literally the app's masonry layout. The brand's motion and the product's main screen are the same gesture. Runs on the existing --dur-panel / --ease-out-strong tokens — no new dependency.

The character has poses

Resting, curious, alert, and tipping over with contents spilling. Grapheine's finding on Bibendum and Benny the Bull was that personality beats appearance — a mascot that does something outlives one that poses. The fourth frame is the retrieval story in one image.

The other five territories

Explored properly, rated honestly. None of these is the answer, but two are worth keeping as secondary devices.

The Swallower

keep as illustration

A capsule hinged open, hands pulling a screen inside. The most energetic thing generated and genuinely funny.

  • Too complex to reduce — dies below about 64px
  • Reads as an egg or a locket before it reads as a capsule
  • Strong for onboarding and marketing illustration, wrong for the mark

Half-Remembered

absorbed into motion

A capsule disintegrating into falling pixels. Conceptually the sharpest of the six — the product's real magic is retrieving something you only half-remember.

  • Not a mascot: no face, no character, and you asked for one
  • The pixels vanish entirely at icon sizes
  • Too good to discard — it became the motion language above

C-as-Crop

smart, timid

The letter C implied by two selection corners and a dashed marquee — literally the gesture of taking a screenshot, and the initial.

  • The idea is better than any execution the model produced
  • Reads as a loading spinner or a selection box, not a letter
  • Zero warmth — the opposite of the "cool fun" brief

Crema

rejected

Espresso pod from above, crema as a spiral of rectangles.

  • Renders as woven rattan, not coffee
  • Says nothing about screenshots — leans entirely on one half of the name
  • Beautiful object, wrong brief

Recommendation

The Loaded Capsule

It passes the rename test — a capsule packed with captured screens cannot be lifted onto another product without becoming meaningless. It gives the brand a colour system for free (the shell stays constant, the contents vary), a mascot with poses, a project device, and a motion language that resolves into the app's own grid.

Still a draft, not an asset. These are generated concepts. The mark needs redrawing by hand as it was last time — but this time there is an idea worth drawing.

If this is approved

  1. Redraw the mark by hand — capsule shell plus exactly three cards, on a 24px grid
  2. Decide the card palette: it becomes a real second tier of the identity, and needs to survive on cream and on #1c1c19
  3. Rebuild the icon set from it — build_icons.py already handles everything downstream of one SVG
  4. Test the 16px case early: three cards may be one too many, and the hinted master may need two
  5. Keep the Swallower as the onboarding illustration and the dissolve as the capture animation

Method note

Mechanisms were built with the Higgsfield brandkit workflow's Design Brain discipline — one concept per direction, each requiring a concrete distinctive element rather than adjectives, screened against its anti-slop list and its rename test. Round one is preserved in board.html and mark/ for comparison.
