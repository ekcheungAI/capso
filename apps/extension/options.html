Capso

Where this extension sends captures.

Use http://localhost:3000 while running the app locally, or your deployed Capso address.

The device code pairs this extension with one browser. Copy it from the Get extension page inside Capso. Without it, a capture could be collected by any other browser you have Capso open in — with it, captures only ever reach this one.

Captures are stored inside this extension the moment you take them and are re-sent until Capso confirms it has them, so closing the app, going offline or restarting the browser cannot lose one.
