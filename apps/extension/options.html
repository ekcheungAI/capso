Capso

Pair this browser with your private Capso library.

Use http://localhost:3000 while running the app locally, or your deployed Capso address.

The device code pairs this extension with the account open on Capso's Get extension page. Capso stores only a one-way fingerprint of the code, and switching accounts produces a different one.

Captures are stored inside this extension the moment you take them and are re-sent until your account confirms the exact capture ID. With a hosted account, delivery no longer needs a Capso tab to stay open.
